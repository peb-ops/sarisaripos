SARI-SARI POS
=============

HOW TO INSTALL (one time only)

  1. Double-click "Install Sari-Sari POS".

     If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC":
     click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

  2. Click OK. The POS opens.

  3. A "Sari-Sari POS" icon is now on your Desktop.
     After this you can delete this folder and the zip file.

EVERY DAY

  Double-click the "Sari-Sari POS" icon on your Desktop.

GOOD TO KNOW

  - No internet needed.
  - Your records are saved on THIS computer only.
  - Make a backup once a week:
      Settings > Download backup
    then copy the file to a USB drive or your phone.
  - To update: install a newer version the same way.
    Your records stay safe.


------------------------------------------------------------
TAGALOG

PAANO I-INSTALL (isang beses lang)

  1. I-double-click ang "Install Sari-Sari POS".

     Kung may lumabas na asul na "Windows protected your PC":
     i-click ang "More info", tapos i-click ang "Run anyway".

  2. I-click ang OK. Magbubukas ang POS.

  3. May "Sari-Sari POS" icon na sa Desktop.
     Puwede mo nang burahin ang folder na ito at ang zip file.

ARAW-ARAW

  I-double-click lang ang "Sari-Sari POS" icon sa Desktop.

DAPAT MONG MALAMAN

  - Hindi kailangan ng internet.
  - Sa computer na ito LANG naka-save ang records mo.
  - Mag-backup isang beses kada linggo:
      Settings > Download backup
    tapos i-copy ang file sa USB o sa phone.
  - Para mag-update: i-install ang bagong version sa parehong paraan.
    Ligtas pa rin ang records mo.
